Frequently Asked Questions & Help

Find quick answers on catalog searching, borrowing requests, password recovery, and fine calculations.

1. Register for an account or log in as a student member.
2. Browse the Catalog or use the instant search bar to find the title you need.
3. Open the book details page and click "Borrow Book" (available copies must be greater than zero).
4. Your request will enter the Pending Requests queue for librarian verification and physical dispatch.

Navigate to My Library (Student Dashboard) and switch to the Active Loans tab. Click the "Return" button next to any active book. Confirm the return date and submit. Your loan record will be marked returned, any overdue fine will be assessed, and available inventory is automatically replenished.

All borrowings carry a standard 14-day loan period. If a book is returned after its due date, an overdue fee of ₹5.00 per late calendar day is assessed. Fines are calculated on the Django server using the exact return timestamp: (Return Date - Due Date) × ₹5.00. You can also simulate calculations using our Due Date Calculator.

Click on "Forgot your password?" on the Login page. Enter the email address associated with your registered member account. A secure one-time cryptographic reset link will be dispatched. Follow the link to enter a new password. In local development or when SMTP credentials are not configured, reset links are printed directly to the server console log for security.
