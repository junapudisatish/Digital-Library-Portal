Circulation Policies & Rules

Standard borrowing terms, overdue regulations, and member responsibilities.

1. Standard Loan Duration (14 Days)

Every approved book issue is granted a default 14-calendar-day borrowing window.

2. Overdue Fines Assessment (₹5.00 / day)

Books returned past the scheduled due date incur an automatic overdue fine of ₹5.00 per overdue calendar day. Formula: max(0, return_date - due_date) × ₹5.00. Books returned on or before the due date incur zero fine.

3. Borrowing Quotas & Eligibility

Registered student members may hold up to 3 concurrent active loans. A member with overdue books or unsettled fines may be restricted from requesting additional titles until outstanding records are cleared.

4. Book Returns & Inventory Replenishment

Upon returning a volume either at the library desk or via student portal self-return, the book's physical copy stock is automatically incremented back to the active catalog in a single atomic transaction.
